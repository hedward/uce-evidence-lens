# License Recommendation — Review Required

## Candidate

MPL-2.0 is a plausible candidate because file-level copyleft can keep modifications to verifier source files available while permitting combination with separately licensed files. It is **not** adopted in this repository.

## Required review before adding `LICENSE`

- Confirm Copyright by UCE's desired inbound/outbound licensing model.
- Review MPL-2.0 patent provisions and termination language with qualified counsel.
- Decide whether challenge rules, deployment platform terms, or third-party public data impose additional notices.
- Confirm ownership and contributor authorization for every newly written file.
- Confirm the public demonstration fixture may be redistributed in its reduced form.
- Review trademark treatment separately; a software license should not imply mark rights.

Alternatives worth counsel comparing are Apache-2.0 (permissive with express patent terms) and MIT (short permissive terms without an express patent license section comparable to Apache-2.0).
