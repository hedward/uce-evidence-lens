Claude integration

Privacy policy

Public references go to the Lens service. Inspection results return to Claude. Original files stay outside this integration.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy describes the UCE Evidence Lens plugin and remote MCP connector for Claude, these information pages under uceevidencelens.com/claude/, and support correspondence. The service is operated by 5 Race Street LLC under its registered alternate names Copyright by UCE and CbyUCE.

This policy does not replace Anthropic’s policies, a public-record source’s policies, or the separate browser Lens workflow’s disclosures. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact on our support page.

Information processed

Public reference and evidence
When Claude calls a Lens tool, our connector receives the public record reference you supplied. It retrieves the corresponding public manifest and approved public chronology sources. Public evidence can include names, titles, rights declarations, assertions about identity, authorship, ownership or consent, hashes, identifiers, and timestamps. The service treats these assertions as untrusted public record text and does not establish that they are true.
Inspection results
We return the reference, public record information, separate check results, limitations, source links, verifier provenance, and inspection time to Claude. Requested reports include the same kinds of information and are unsigned.
Connection information
Hosting providers process network requests and associated metadata to deliver and protect the connector and website. This can include IP addresses, request times, routes or URLs, response status, user-agent or client information, and other ordinary protocol metadata. A server-to-server tool call may expose a Claude host’s network address rather than your own.
Support correspondence
If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Send only what is needed to understand your request.

Purpose and recipients

We use public references and evidence to perform the requested inspection or generate its report. We use operational information to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service, and support correspondence to respond to your request.

  • Anthropic and Claude handle your conversation, tool input, and returned results under the policies and controls applicable to your Claude account, plan, and workspace. The standalone plugin does not independently call the Anthropic API. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Google Cloud hosts the connector backend in the United States (us-central1) and processes service requests and operational information. See the Google Cloud Privacy Notice.
  • Cloudflare serves these public website pages and processes website requests. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • CbyUCE and public Arweave services receive server requests for the selected public identifiers. CbyUCE is operated by the same company as this connector. Approved Arweave and transaction-derived Arweave gateways provide public records and chronology information. Turbo Gateway may provide transaction status, block, and bundled-item parent relationships. These requests contain public identifiers and do not include original file bytes, caller cookies, or credentials. Those services may keep their own request records.
  • GitHub hosts the public plugin source. Visiting the repository or choosing to download or manually install from it discloses request and any GitHub account information to GitHub under its General Privacy Statement. Source access through GitHub is optional.
  • Our email provider processes correspondence you send to the published support address.

The connector does not sell personal information or use inspection data for advertising. These documentation pages include no scripts, forms, advertising code, or application analytics. The connector has no user-account store or application analytics.

Storage and retention

The Lens application processes inspection data in memory for each request. It has no inspection database, persistent report store, user-account store, or application cookies. It does not intentionally log request bodies, public references, inspection results, or conversation text. Generated reports return to Claude; saving or sharing one is your choice.

For the current connector deployment, Cloud Run request logs are excluded from our project’s default log sink. Ordinary application and build logs in that project have a seven-day retention setting. Required audit records, security and operational records, and provider records follow their separate retention rules. These controls do not mean Google, Anthropic, Cloudflare, GitHub, public sources, or another provider keeps no connection metadata.

Claude conversations, public source records, GitHub activity, and any report you save follow their own retention arrangements; the seven-day setting does not apply to them. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and related security or legal obligations. You may ask us about or request deletion of correspondence through support.

Your choices and requests

  • Use only public references you intend to disclose to Claude and the connector. Public records can still contain personal information.
  • Do not send original works, private files or manifests, credentials, payment information, or sensitive personal information. Lens does not need them.
  • Disable or remove the plugin or connector to stop future tool calls. Use your Claude account or workspace controls for conversation and account data.
  • Contact us for access, correction, deletion, or another privacy request relating to information we control. Available rights depend on applicable law, and we may need enough information to verify and handle the request.

We cannot alter or erase information already published in a public UCE record, public blockchain or gateway, or data controlled by Anthropic or another provider. Contact the relevant service for those requests.

Email privacy questions or requests to support@universalcreationevidence.com.

Security and changes

The connector restricts retrieval destinations, limits request and response sizes, uses timeouts, rejects unsafe redirects, and isolates inspection requests. No internet service can promise absolute security or uninterrupted availability.

We will update this page when the service, providers, legal requirements, or data practices change and revise the date above. Contact Lens support with questions about this policy.
