ChatGPT integration

Privacy policy

Public references go to the Lens service. Inspection results return to ChatGPT. Original files stay outside this integration.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Scope and operator

This policy describes the UCE Evidence Lens ChatGPT integration and its information pages under uceevidencelens.com/gpt/, operated by 5 Race Street LLC under its registered alternate names Copyright by UCE and CbyUCE. This policy does not replace OpenAI’s policies, a public record publisher’s policies, or the separate browser Lens workflow’s disclosures.

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact on our support page.

Information processed

Public reference and evidence
When ChatGPT calls a Lens tool, our service receives the supplied public record reference. It retrieves the corresponding public manifest and available transaction metadata. Public evidence can include names, titles, rights declarations, ownership or consent assertions, hashes, identifiers, and timestamps.
Inspection results
We return the reference, public record information, separate check results, limitations, source links, and inspection time to ChatGPT. Requested reports include the same kinds of information and are unsigned.
Connection information
Hosting providers process network requests and associated metadata to deliver and protect the service. This can include IP addresses, request times, URLs, status codes, and browser or client information. A server-to-server call may expose the ChatGPT host’s network address rather than your own.
Support correspondence
If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Send only what is needed to understand your request.

Purpose and recipients

We use public references and evidence to perform the requested inspection or generate its report. We use operational information to run, secure, and troubleshoot the service, and support correspondence to respond to your request.

  • OpenAI / ChatGPT handles your conversation, tool input, and returned results under the policies and controls applicable to your account. The integration does not call the OpenAI API independently.
  • Google Cloud hosts the Lens backend in the United States (us-central1) and processes service requests and operational information.
  • Cloudflare serves these public website pages and processes website requests.
  • CbyUCE and public Arweave gateways receive requests for the selected public identifiers from our server. A bundled Arweave item may also require a public parent-transaction lookup through Turbo Gateway. Status and block checks may also use Turbo Gateway when the primary gateway is rate limited or unavailable. These requests contain public identifiers and do not include original file bytes, caller cookies, or credentials. Those services may maintain their own request records.
  • Our email provider processes correspondence you send to the published support address.

These integration pages and the embedded ChatGPT component contain no advertising or analytics code, and the integration does not sell personal information or use inspection data for advertising. Provider handling is governed by their applicable terms and policies, including OpenAI’s privacy policy, Google Cloud’s privacy notice, and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Storage and retention

The Lens application processes inspections in memory. It has no database of inspections, user-account store, persistent report store, or application cookies. It does not intentionally log request bodies, public references, or inspection results.

For the current backend deployment, Cloud Run request logs are excluded from our project’s default log sink. Ordinary application and build logs in that project have a seven-day retention setting. Required audit logs and provider operational records follow their applicable retention rules. This is not a promise that Google or other providers retain no connection metadata.

ChatGPT conversations, public source records, and any report you save follow their own retention arrangements; the seven-day setting does not apply to them. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and any related security or legal obligations. You can ask us about or request deletion of correspondence through support.

Your choices and requests

  • Use only public references you intend to disclose to ChatGPT and the service. Public records can still contain personal information.
  • Do not send original works, private files, credentials, payment information, or sensitive personal information. Lens does not need them.
  • Disable or remove the Lens connection to stop future tool calls. Use ChatGPT’s account controls for conversation and account data.
  • Manage saved reports on your device. Following an “Open in Evidence Lens” link starts the separate browser workflow.
  • Contact us for access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests relating to information we control. Available rights depend on applicable law, and we may need enough information to verify and handle your request.

We cannot alter or erase public records held by CbyUCE, Arweave, or other parties, or delete data held in your ChatGPT account. Contact the relevant service for those requests.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the integration’s data practices change and revise the date above. Contact us through Lens support with questions about this policy.
