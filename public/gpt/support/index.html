Common questions

Where can I install it?
The integration is in developer preview and has not been approved or published in the ChatGPT directory. See the preview instructions. Testing inside ChatGPT is still pending. Developer-mode availability depends on your ChatGPT account and workspace.
Why is a check unavailable or retryable?
A public source may be rate limited, unavailable, or too slow to respond. Arweave rate limiting observed on September 30, 2026 affects this preview. Retry later; an unavailable check has not passed. Another completed check may still have a useful result.
Why is a format or check unsupported?
Lens only claims support for reviewed formats and check rules. Unknown formats and hybrid 2.x cryptographic checks remain unsupported. Some historical Standard hash results cannot be resolved from the public fields.
What if the reference is rejected?
Use a public CbyUCE verification link or 64-character record hash, or an Arweave manifest transaction link or 43-character ID. Private links, arbitrary websites, pasted manifests, and files are outside the ChatGPT tools’ accepted inputs.
How do I compare my original file?
Use the separate browser-based Evidence Lens and its local-file comparison. Do not attach the original to the ChatGPT integration.
Why can’t I save a report?
Download support depends on the ChatGPT host. The widget also displays the report as JSON text. A report remains an unsigned dated inspection, not a UCE Certificate.
Can Lens fix or delete a public record?
No. Lens reads public evidence. Contact the record’s publisher or hosting service about corrections or removal; public or permanent records may not be removable.
Does a verified result prove ownership?
No. Review exactly which integrity or chronology check passed and what it covers. Identity, authorship, ownership, rights, and consent statements remain recorded assertions.
